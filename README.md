# Sales-Revenue-Dashboard
An interactive Sales &amp; Revenue Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze business performance and uncover meaningful sales insights.
Sales & Revenue Dashboard

An interactive Power BI Sales & Revenue Dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and sales trends.

The dashboard provides a detailed view of business performance through key KPIs such as Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Ship Days. It also includes regional, category, sub-category, payment mode, segment, ship mode, and state-level analysis.

Dashboard Highlights
Sales & Profit Analysis – Monitor overall sales and profitability.
Regional Analysis – Compare sales performance across Central, East, South, and West regions.
Year-over-Year Trends – Analyze monthly sales and profit trends for 2019 and 2020.
Category Analysis – Compare performance across Office Supplies, Technology, and Furniture.
Payment Mode Analysis – Analyze sales distribution across Online, COD, and Card payments.
Segment Analysis – Compare Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
Ship Mode Analysis – Evaluate sales across Standard Class, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day shipping.
Sub-Category Analysis – Identify high-performing sub-categories such as Phones, Chairs, and Binders.
State-Level Analysis – Identify states contributing the highest sales.
Sales Forecasting – Includes a 15-day sales forecast to provide a short-term view of expected sales performance.

Objective

The objective of this project is to transform sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps identify sales trends, profitable segments, high-performing products and regions, and future sales patterns.

The dashboard is designed to support data-driven business decision-making by bringing multiple dimensions of sales performance into a single interactive report.
